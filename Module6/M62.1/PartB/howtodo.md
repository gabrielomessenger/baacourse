1 Policy principles

Describe the organisation’s commitment to fair and compliant subcontractor selection.

Include:

- compliance with legislation
- WHS obligations
- fair procurement processes

------------------------------------------------

2 Selection and engagement procedure

Describe the process for selecting subcontractors.

Include steps such as:

• identifying required subcontractor trades
• checking the project sequence and when each trade is needed
• issuing invitations to tender or requesting quotes
• reviewing contractor submissions
• evaluating contractor capability, price, experience, licences and insurance
• selecting the most appropriate subcontractor
• engaging critical trades early enough to protect project deadlines

------------------------------------------------

3 Equal opportunity in subcontractor selection

Explain how the selection process ensures:

• fair treatment of all applicants
• transparent evaluation criteria
• non-discriminatory contractor engagement
• consistent review process for each applicant

------------------------------------------------

Describe verification processes including:

• trade licences
• insurance certificates
• contractor experience
• references
• WHS compliance history
• financial or capacity checks if relevant
• ability to meet program timing

------------------------------------------------

5 Notification of tender outcomes

Describe how the builder will:

• notify successful subcontractors
• notify unsuccessful tenderers
• record and document the selection decision
• confirm scope, start dates and expectations with the successful subcontractor

------------------------------------------------

6 Commencement and site induction process

Explain procedures before subcontractors begin work, including:

• contract signing

• site induction
• WHS briefing
• environmental requirements
• communication procedures
