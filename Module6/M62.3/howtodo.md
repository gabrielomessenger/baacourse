## STEP 2 -- UNDERSTAND THE ASSIGNMENT

This assignment requires preparing a **Construction Environmental Management Plan (CEMP)**.

The purpose of the plan is to explain:

• How environmental impacts will be identified  
• How environmental risks will be controlled  
• How construction activities will comply with environmental regulations  
• How environmental monitoring will be performed during the project

The CEMP should demonstrate professional environmental management
practices for a construction project.

You may base the plan on:

• Your own construction project  
OR  
• The sample project provided in the course materials.

## STEP 4 -- STRUCTURE OF THE CEMP

The Construction Environmental Management Plan must follow the structure below.

### 1. Abstract

Provide a short overview of the environmental management plan,
including the purpose of the document and the project context.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2. Project Background

Describe the construction project.

Include:

• Type of construction project  
• Project location  
• Project scope  
• General construction activities involved.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 3. Purpose of the Construction Environmental Management Plan

Explain why the environmental management plan is required.

For example:

• To minimise environmental impact  
• To comply with environmental legislation  
• To establish monitoring and control procedures.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 4. Project Scope and Objectives

Describe the environmental objectives of the project.

Examples include:

• Prevent environmental pollution  
• Reduce noise and dust emissions  
• Proper waste management  
• Protection of soil and water resources.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 5. Key Members of the Project Team

Identify key personnel responsible for environmental management.

Examples:

• Project Manager  
• Site Supervisor  
• Environmental Officer  
• Contractors and subcontractors.

Explain their responsibilities.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 6. Relevant Legislation and Australian Standards

Describe legislation and regulations relevant to environmental
management in construction.

Include sections for:

A. Acoustics  
B. Air quality  
C. Demolition regulations  
D. Emergency procedures  
E. Environmental auditing  
F. Storage and handling of materials  
G. Commonwealth legislation  
H. Other relevant federal legislation  
I. State environmental legislation  
J. External environmental codes of practice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 7. Environmental Indicators

Identify indicators that may show environmental impact.

Examples include:

• Excess dust levels  
• Increased noise levels  
• Water contamination  
• Soil erosion  
• Waste accumulation.

Also identify **potential causes of environmental impact**.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 8. Risk Assessment

Identify environmental risks associated with construction activities.

For each risk include:

• Source of risk  
• Possible environmental impact  
• Likelihood of occurrence  
• Mitigation measures.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 9. Data Gathering and Control Measures

Explain how environmental conditions will be monitored and controlled.

Include sections for:

• Air quality  
• Soil quality  
• Water quality  
• Noise emissions  
• Waste management.

Provide examples of control measures such as:

• Dust suppression  
• Sediment control barriers  
• Noise restrictions  
• Waste segregation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 10. Training and Awareness

Explain how workers and contractors will be trained in environmental
responsibilities.

Examples include:

• Environmental induction training  
• Toolbox meetings  
• Environmental awareness programs.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 11. Communication Strategy

Explain how environmental issues will be communicated within the project.

Include:

• Reporting procedures  
• Communication between contractors and management.

Also include:

#### Incident Reporting

Explain how environmental incidents will be reported and recorded.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 12. Environmental Audit Procedures

Describe how environmental compliance will be audited.

Examples include:

• Regular site inspections  
• Environmental performance reviews  
• Compliance checks with regulations.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 13. Change Management Procedures

Explain how environmental procedures will be updated if project
conditions change.

Include documentation and approval processes.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 14. Emergency and Remediation Response

Describe procedures for responding to environmental emergencies.

Examples include:

• Chemical spills  
• Fuel leaks  
• Water contamination  
• Dust control failures.

Explain remediation actions.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 15. Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)

Explain how environmental management integrates with workplace
health and safety practices.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 16. Review Processes and Plan Updates

Explain how the environmental management plan will be reviewed and
updated during the project.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 17. SWOT Analysis

Provide a SWOT analysis of the environmental management approach.

Include:

• Strengths  
• Weaknesses  
• Opportunities  
• Threats.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 18. Monitoring

Explain how environmental performance will be monitored throughout
the project.

Include monitoring schedules and responsibilities.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 19. Conclusion

Summarise how the environmental management plan supports responsible
construction practices and regulatory compliance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 20. References

Provide references for:

• Environmental legislation  
• Australian standards  
• Environmental guidelines  
• Government environmental policies.
